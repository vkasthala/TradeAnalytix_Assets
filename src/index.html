Trading Plan

Prior to market opening, users can create a trading plan and later, post-market hours, they can review their executed trades, notes, statistics, and any valuable insights gained from that day's trades.

Trade Builder

Users can create trades and analyze them based on risk and statistics. They can also add notes, attach documents, and check if they align with the self-set trading rules.


Payoff Analyzer

Enables users to simulate future scenarios and analyze potential returns on option trade strategies. Users can adjust their trades until they align with their desired risk level and return expectations.

Trade Journal

Users can keep a journal of their trades by importing them manually or automatically from their brokerages. They can also add notes, attach documents, and indicate whether the trades aligned with their self-set trading rules.

Trading Rules

Users can set manual or automatic trading rules based on their experience and best practices. Aligning trades with these rules helps avoid repeating common mistakes.

Analytics

Users can track their trading performance over time with reports and analytics. By identifying strengths and limitations in a historical context, users can make better trading decisions in the future.

Bulk Update

Users can add notes for multiple trades using an intuitive Excel-like user interface. This interface enables updating journal details for multiple trades simultaneously from a single page, without having to open each trade separately.

Frequently Asked Questions

CueTrade is meant for all those traders who struggle to maintain discipline. Indiscipline includes trading without rationale, excessive trading, taking excessive risk, lack of control over emotions, etc. It helps such traders adopt a systematic approach to trading by allowing them to create a detailed trading plan before the market opens and reviewing their trades with reports and insights after the market closes so that they can identify their mistakes and learn from them. Also, it offers the ability to set rules so that traders can align their trades with the self-set rules.

You can use CueTrade before and after the market hours. Before the market opens, you can create a trading plan and analyze trades using risk and statistical measures to make informed decisions. After the market closes, you can write notes on your trades and review analytics from your trading history to update your rules and plan for the next trading day.

Our goal is to make journaling simple and effortless. We know that recording data for each trade can be a hassle, and that's why many traders don't journal their trades even though it's beneficial. CueTrade saves you time by automatically calculating some parameters of your trades, so you don't have to enter them manually. With CueTrade, you can take notes on multiple trades in a single easy-to-use interface, so you don't have to update each trade individually.

With CueTrade, you can create trading rules based on your experience or best practices. These rules help you to stay within your boundaries and avoid making the same mistakes. There are two types of rules: automatic and manual. Automatic rules are checked for every trade, and CueTrade shows if the trade is aligned with the rule or not. Manual rules are text- based reminders that you need to update manually.

CueTrade is a unique trade journal that caters to retail traders. It offers several features that set it apart from other journals:

  • CueTrade helps traders analyze their trades from various perspectives like rationale, statistics, risk, and emotions.
  • It allows users to set up automatic and manual trading rules to ensure that their trades align with these rules.
  • CueTrade groups all trades on a symbol into a trade strategy, making it easier for users to enter notes, assess risk, review stats, and check alignment with rules at the trade strategy level instead of individual trades.
  • You can provide notes on multiple trades in a single, user-friendly interface similar to an Excel sheet.
  • CueTrade prioritizes risk management, and its Payoff Analyzer tool helps traders analyze the potential profit or loss of trades involving options at a specific date in the future by varying stock price and implied volatility.

You can add trades to CueTrade from your brokerage account in three ways:

  • Connect your brokerage account with CueTrade if it's supported, and the trade history will be automatically imported every day.
  • For tdAmeritrade or Zerodha, download trade history files from your brokerage and upload them to CueTrade's Import Trades page.
  • For unsupported brokerages, download the trade history files, add trades to CueTrade's standard template, and upload it to the Import Trades page.

CueTrade offers stats and reports in several categories, such as performance, risk management, commissions, goals, and discipline. CueTrade offers over 40 reports, and the list is expanding. These reports are based on your trading history and journal details.

Payoff analyzer tool in CueTrade helps you analyze option-based trade strategies to determine their potential profit or loss at a future date, given a particular stock price and implied volatility. You can adjust the stock price, implied volatility, and days to expiration to see how they impact the potential profit or loss. This helps you make informed decisions on whether to modify the trade strategy to align it with your risk tolerance and return expectations. The best part is, the tool is straightforward and user-friendly.
