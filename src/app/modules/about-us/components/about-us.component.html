Welcome to CueTrade!

I'm Venkat Kasthala, the founder of CueTrade.

A few years ago, driven by a passion for transforming the way traders approach the markets, I embarked on a mission to create a solution that addresses the pervasive challenges faced by active traders.

The reality is that over 90% of active traders encounter issues such as impulsive decisions, rule-breaking, and overtrading, resulting in substantial losses. The easy accessibility of trading tools, margin funds, and the influence of social media contribute to a lack of discipline, turning trading into a risky endeavor. CueTrade was born as a response to empower traders by instilling clear goals, effective rules, and a disciplined daily routine involving planning, journaling, and performance analysis.

CueTrade is the result of nearly two years of dedication from a team committed to revolutionizing the trading experience. Each team member, including myself, contributed part-time efforts to bring CueTrade to life.

CueTrade takes a unique stance, advocating for a systematic approach to trading. We encourage less frequent trading, emphasizing planning, analyzing trades, comprehensive journaling, and reviewing performance. The platform is not just a tool; it's a reflection of personal experiences, trading best practices, and the shared concerns of retail traders.

The beta version of CueTrade is now live, offering complimentary access to active traders. Join us in refining the platform for a more disciplined and successful trading experience.

For inquiries or a demo request, reach out to us at support@cuetrade.io.
