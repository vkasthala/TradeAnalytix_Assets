Welcome to CueTrade!

I'm Venkat Kasthala, the founder of CueTrade.

Founded with the vision of helping traders protect their capital and achieve sustainable financial outcomes, CueTrade is for traders who want to take control of their financial future. Whether you're an experienced trader or just starting out, CueTrade empowers you to trade smarter and stay aligned with your goals.

The reality is that over 90% of traders lose money, primarily due to a lack of discipline, which impacts both their finances and overall well-being. Despite having access to advanced trading tools, the challenge persists, with millions entering the market unprepared each year. This widespread problem underscores a critical need for a trading platform that instills a disciplined approach essential for sustained success.

At CueTrade, we are on a mission to change this. Our platform is designed to help retail traders approach the financial markets with discipline, reduce costly mistakes, and make smarter, data-driven decisions. CueTrade offers powerful tools, including real-time order validation of rules, trading plans, trading journals, and comprehensive analytics, to give you confidence in every trade.

The beta version of CueTrade is now live, offering complimentary access to selected traders. Join us in refining the platform for a more disciplined and successful trading experience. For inquiries or demo requests, feel free to reach out at support@cuetrade.io.
