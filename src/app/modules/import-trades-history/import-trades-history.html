Add your trades from your brokerage to CueTrade in two ways: Manual Import or Auto-Import. If your brokerage supports auto-import, you link your brokerage account to CueTrade and the trades you execute in the brokerage from that juncture will added to CueTrade automatically. If you prefer manual import, you upload the trade history files from your brokerage and your trades would be added to CueTrade.
