Instructions for Adding Zerodha Trades to CueTrade:
  • Log in to Kite trading platform (https://kite.zerodha.com)
  • Access the user menu and choose Console or login directly to Kite Console (https://console.zerodha.com)
  • Access the Reports menu and choose Tradebook
  • Download Equity transactions as follows
    • Choose Equity from Segment dropdown, select the date range, and click submit
    • Click the Download CSV option to save the equity transactions in CSV format.
  • Download F&O transactions as follows
    • Choose F&O from Segment dropdown, select the date range, and click submit
    • Click the Download CSV option to save the F&O transactions in CSV format.
Instructions for Adding Zerodha Trades to CueTrade:
  • Navigate to the Import Trades page within CueTrade.
  • From the Select Brokerage dropdown, opt for Zerodha.
  • Choose the Equity file that you downloaded from Zerodha in step 4 above.
  • Choose the F&O file that you downloaded from Zerodha in step 5 above.
  • Click the Upload button to initiate the import process.
  • Your trades from Robinhood will now be accessible in CueTrade. To view them, go to the Positions page.
