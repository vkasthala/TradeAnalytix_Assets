Trading Rules
We all want to be disciplined and not repeat the same mistakes we made in the past. But, the mercurial nature of the markets, ease of trading through mobile devices, and continuous influx of trading ideas through social media makes it very difficult to be disciplined and influences one to take impromptu decisions. Rules feature set helps resolve this issue for the traders. One can create manual or automated rules within the system and whenever their actions do not align with these rules, they will be notified at the right time thereby helping them operate within the rules.
