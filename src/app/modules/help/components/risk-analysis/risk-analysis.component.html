Risk Analysis
Everyone who traded in options must have witnessed that option price doesn’t always move in line with the underlying stock prices. Sometimes, price of an option may not move much or even go down even when the stock goes up significantly. Estimating potential returns on a trade strategy involving options is extremely hard, because there are so many things at play in option pricing including the strike price, expiration date, and implied volatility.

Risk analysis feature in CueTrade allows users analyze the potential returns on a trade strategy involving options at present and in the future while varying stock price, implied volatility, and days to expiration. Based on the analysis results, users can modify the trade strategy until they are comfortable with the risk-reward potential of the trade.

Here’s how users can perform risk analysis within CueTrade.
Click on Risk Analysis link in the main menu to navigate to the Risk Analysis page.


Enter the first few letters of the symbol on which you would like to perform risk analysis. App will show all the matching symbols so that you can select appropriate symbol.


One a symbol is chosen from the list, click on Submit to move to the risk analysis page. In this page, you can build the trade strategy comprising of stocks and/or options that you would like to analyze.


Click on Add Stock button to add a stock leg. Select Action and enter the Quantity and Price.


If you have any options to be added to the trade strategy, click on Add Option button and provide the required details: Action, Contracts, Strike Price, Option Type, Expiry Date, and Price. Amount will be auto calculated by the system.


Up to four options can be added to the trade strategy. Click Next to proceed further.


Click Next button to proceed with the risk analysis. System will calculate the implied volatility and days left for expiry of all the options.


Click Analyze button to look at the payoff of the trade strategy in a data table.


In the data table, the potential return of the stock and options for the selected stock price range is displayed. User can update the stock price range so that the potential return of the trade for the selected price range is displayed in the data table. For example, in the below image stock price range is changed from -5% to 15% and the data table displayed potential return of the trade when the stock moves between -5% to 15%.

User can modify the implied volatility and the days to expiration and click Analyze button to view the updated results.


Click on Charts tab to see the potential returns of the trade strategy in synchronized charts. As you move the cursor over one chart, returns on all your positions at the price point are displayed.


Click on Max Profit/Risk button to determine the maximum risk and maximum profit of the trade strategy at the specified implied volatility levels and days to expiration.


At any point in time, you can click on Create Strategy button to create add this as a trade in the system.


Upon clicking this button, user will be navigated to Add Trade page. The trade details entered in Risk Analysis are retained in Add Trade page. User can click on Add Trade to add this a trade in the system.
