Data Setup
You know how much money you made or lost in a certain period, but do you know what kind of trades worked well for you and what didn’t during the same period? Probably not! CueTrade provides in-depth reports and metrics on your trading history. Reports are provided on your portfolio, performance, risk management, discipline, rules, and goals. With this, one can build on their strengths by doing more of what worked in the past and avoiding kind of trades that didn’t work
