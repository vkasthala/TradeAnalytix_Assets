What is TradeAnalytix?

clear
TradeAnalytix is a new age trade planner, journal and decision support tool for retail investors and traders. The app provides users with perspective to plan trades, journal their trades and analyze the data to identify where they need improvement.

TradeAnalytix as a Trade Planner

clear
TradeAnalytix is a Trade Planner for the users who use it before executing trades in the brokerage. Our TradeAnalytix product brings transparency to the chaotic trading world in utilizing its features. It gives an advantage in making a precise trade decision and helps you avoid errors due to emotions. From novice to expert traders, everyone can benefit from our TradeAnalytix product!

TradeAnalytix as a Trade Journal

clear
TradeAnalytix is a Trade Journal for the users who use it after executing trades in the brokerage. It is a digital journal to keep track of trades executed over a period of time along with hypothesis, rules, and charts to understand trends.
