What is TradeAnalytix?

clear
TradeAnalytix is a new age trade planner, journal and decision support tool for retail investors and traders.

The app provides you with the perspective to plan your trades, journal your trades and analyze the data to find out your strengths and limitations as a trader and make informed decisions.

TradeAnalytix as a Trade Planner

clear
Users can plan their trades in TradeAnalytix before executing the trades in the brokerage platforms. Such planning gives you an advantage in making a trade decision and helps you avoid mistakes due to emotions.

TradeAnalytix as a Trade Journal

clear
Users can journal their trades in TradeAnalytix after executing the trades in the brokerage platforms. Trades can be added either individually or in bulk by importing trade history files.
