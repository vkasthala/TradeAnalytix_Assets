What is TradeAnalytix?

clear
TradeAnalytix is a new age trade planner, journal and decision support tool for retail investors and traders. The app provides you with the perspective to plan your trades, journal your trades and analyze the data to find out your strengths and limitations as a trader and make informed decisions.

TradeAnalytix as a Trade Planner

clear
TradeAnalytix is a Trade Planner for the users who use it before executing trades in their brokerage account. It gives you an advantage in making a trade decision and helps you avoid errors due to emotions. From novice to expert traders, everyone can benefit from our TradeAnalytix product.

TradeAnalytix as a Trade Journal

clear
TradeAnalytix is a Trade Journal for the users who use it after executing trades in your brokerage account. It is a digital journal to keep track of trades executed over a period along with hypothesis, rules, and charts to understand trends.
