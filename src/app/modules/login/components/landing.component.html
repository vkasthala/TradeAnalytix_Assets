Prepare for the day ahead with a meticulous trade plan
A trade plan created before the market hours allows you to think objectively about the market without getting influenced by the price movements. Within a trade plan, one can record their perspective on the market and intended actions during the day. Improve trading decisions through in-depth reports on trade history
Identify your strengths and weaknesses by reviewing reports and metrics derived from your trading journal and history. Reports are generated across different categories such as portfolio, performance, risk, discipline, trading rules, and goals. Build on your strengths by doing more of what worked in the past and avoiding the kind of trades that didn’t work.Read More
Set trading rules and abide by them to avoid repeating the mistakes
Market volatility, ease of trading, and influx of trading ideas makes it difficult to be disciplined and influences one to take impromptu decisions. Rules feature allows you to set trading rules (manual and automatic) and trade as per these rules so that past mistakes can be avoided. Manual rules are the notes that you would like to be reminded of, while opening or closing positions. Automatic rules are the rules that trigger alerts or notifications when they are broken.Read More
Analyze risk of complex trades by simulating possible future scenarios
Analyze the potential returns on a trade strategy involving options at present and in the future while varying stock price, implied volatility, and days to expiration. Risk analysis feature allows you to modify the trade strategy and keep assessing its profitability until you arrive at a trade that is aligned with your risk appetite.Read More
Pick the right strategy that is aligned with your risk appetite
Let's say you are bullish on a stock. Do you know which bullish strategy on the stock works well for you within your risk appetite? Strategy Comparison feature allows you to compare up to five different strategies on a symbol and analyze their profitability in different scenarios so that you can pick a strategy that aligned with your risk appetite.
