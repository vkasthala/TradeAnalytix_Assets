Record your trades in detail with a comprehensive trade journal
Adopt a methodical approach to trading by recording your trades with detailed trade thesis. User can record the justification for the trade, source of the idea, events surrounding the trade, planned or impromptu trade, target price, target holding period, mindset, and technical indicator used. Supporting documents or images can be attached as well.Read More
Prepare for the day ahead with the creation of a trading plan
A trade plan created before the market hours allows you to think objectively about the market without getting influenced by the price movements. User can create a trade plan for the day by logging his perspective on the market, entering notes against open positions, adding any new stocks that he intends to trade, etc. Trade plan can be updated aftermarket hours to check alignment with the actual plan and record lessons learnt on that day.Read More
Improve trading decisions with in-depth reporting on trade history
You know how much money you made or lost in a certain period, but do you know what kind of trades worked well for you and what didn’t during the same period? Probably not! CueTrade provides in-depth reports and metrics on your trading history. Reports are provided on your portfolio, performance, risk management, discipline, rules, and goals. With this, one can build on their strengths by doing more of what worked in the past and avoiding kind of trades that didn’t work.Read More
Set manual or automated rules and align your trades with them
We all want to be disciplined and not repeat the same mistakes we made in the past. But, the mercurial nature of the markets, ease of trading through mobile devices, and continuous influx of trading ideas through social media makes it very difficult to be disciplined and influences one to take impromptu decisions. Rules feature set helps resolve this issue for the traders. One can create manual or automated rules within the system and whenever their actions do not align with these rules, they will be notified at the right time thereby helping them operate within the rules.Read More
Analyze risk of complex trades by simulating possible future scenarios
Do you know what would be the profitability of your trades involving options in the future at a different stock price and implied volatility? If not, risk analysis feature is what you should consider because it allows you to analyze the risk of a trade strategy while varying implied volatility, days left for expiration, and stock price. The feature allows you to assess the risk, modify your trades, re-assess the risk until you arrive at the trade strategy that you are comfortable with.Read More
Pick the right strategy that is aligned with your risk appetite
Let's say you are bullish on a stock. Do you know which bullish strategy on the stock works well for you within your risk appetite? Strategy Comparison feature allows you to compare up to five different strategies on a stock and analyze the risk and profitability in different scenarios so that you can pick a strategy that aligned with your risk appetite.Read More
Import historical trades from your brokerage on the click of a button
Upload trade history files from your brokerages to add the trades to CueTrade in bulk without having to add one by one through the user interface. Upon successful import, trades are created, updated or closed in the system and strategies are assigned to the trades. Users can update journal details of the imported trades in bulk as well.
