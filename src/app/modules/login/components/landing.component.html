Cuetrade Features

Daily Trading Plan

Users can proactively create a trading plan everyday before the market opens. After the market closes, they can conveniently review their executed trades, study notes, analyze statistics, and gain valuable insights from the day's trading activities.

In-depth Trade Analysis

:Enables traders to conduct thorough analysis of their trades from multiple perspectives. Traders can delve into the rationale behind their decisions, examine relevant statistics, assess risk factors, and evaluate the emotional aspects involved.

Option Payoff Analysis

Assess profitability of option strategies at a specific future date within a desired stock price range while adjusting implied volatility and days to expiration. Gain insights into the impact of these adjustments and align your trade with risk tolerance and return expectations.

Extensive Trading Journal

Allows users to effortlessly maintain a record of their trades. Import trades from brokerages, add notes, attach documents, and indicate alignment with trading rules. This feature keeps track of trading activities, providing valuable insights for analysis.

Personalized Trading Rules

Enables you to define personalized trading rules that serve as invaluable guides, keeping you within your boundaries and preventing repetitive mistakes. Automatic rules are verified for each trade indicating compliance instantly while manual rules act as text-based reminders.

Analytics

Offers a wide array of statistics and reports, providing valuable insights into your trading performance covering essential aspects like performance, risk management, commissions, goals, and discipline. These reports are based on your trading history and journal details.

Effortless Trade Import

Trades can be added to CueTrade manually by uploading trade history files exported from your brokerage. With auto-import, you link your brokerage with CueTrade enabling all your trades from the brokerage to be automatically added to CueTrade.

Bulk Journal

Bulk Journal offers an intuitive user interface resembling an excel spreadsheet that allows you to effortlessly review trade strategies, add notes, and update journal details for multiple trades at once, eliminating the need to update each trade individually.

Daily Tracker

CueTrade focuses on three daily steps to improve as a trader: Plan, Journal, and Review. Daily Tracker fosters trading discipline by monitoring daily task progress effortlessly. Tasks are automatically marked as complete based on your activities, simplifying tracking.

Frequently Asked Questions

CueTrade is ideal for traders who desire greater discipline in their trading endeavors. If you find yourself struggling with impulsive trading decisions, excessive risk-taking, lack of emotional control, or trading without a clear rationale, CueTrade is the perfect solution for you. CueTrade helps you become a better trader by adopting a systematic approach through planning, journaling, and tracking your trading performance over time.

CueTrade can be used to optimize your trading efficiency throughout the day. It is beneficial to utilize CueTrade before the market opens to create a comprehensive trading plan and conduct thorough analysis of potential trades from various perspectives, enabling you to make informed decisions.


After the market closes, CueTrade proves valuable for reviewing your trades, adding notes, ensuring alignment with your trading rules, and leveraging insightful reports. This post-market analysis helps you pinpoint mistakes and gain valuable learning experiences to enhance your trading strategies.

CueTrade aims to simplify the journaling process, recognizing that the manual effort involved can discourage traders from adopting this beneficial practice. To address these challenges, CueTrade offers the following solutions:

- Enables effortless trade import from brokerage accounts, with automatic and manual integration options.

- Offers an intuitive interface for taking notes on multiple trade strategies in one place without the need to update each trade individually.

- Groups related trades into strategies automatically, enabling comprehensive analysis and journaling at strategy level.

- Automates the calculation of trade parameters such as strategy and direction eliminating the need for manual entry.

CueTrade enables you to define trading rules that align with your experience and best practices. These rules serve as invaluable guides, keeping you within your boundaries and preventing repetitive mistakes.


CueTrade offers two types of rules: automatic and manual. Automatic rules are consistently verified for each trade, with CueTrade promptly indicating whether a trade complies with the rule or not. On the other hand, manual rules act as text-based reminders, requiring manual updates as needed.

Adding trades to CueTrade from your brokerage account is a breeze, and you have two convenient options:


Auto-Import: Seamlessly connect your brokerage account to CueTrade, allowing for automatic importation of all your trades from the brokerage directly into CueTrade.


Manual Import: Simply download the trade history files from your brokerage account and effortlessly upload them into CueTrade. This allows you to add your trades to CueTrade manually.

CueTrade presents an extensive range of statistics and reports across various categories, enabling traders to gain valuable insights into their trading performance. With a continually growing list of over reports and statistics, CueTrade ensures comprehensive coverage of essential aspects such as performance, risk management, commissions, goals, and discipline. These insightful reports are meticulously generated based on your trading history and journal details, offering a comprehensive analysis of your trading activities.

CueTrade's Payoff Analyzer tool is an invaluable resource for analyzing option-based trade strategies, allowing you to assess their potential profit or loss at a specific future date. With the ability to input a desired stock price and adjust variables like implied volatility and days to expiration, you gain precise insights into how these adjustments impact potential outcomes. By understanding how adjustments to the trade strategy affect potential profit or loss, you can align your trading approach with your risk tolerance and return expectations.
