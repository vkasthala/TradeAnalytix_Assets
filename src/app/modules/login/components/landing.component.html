Product Benefits

Adopt a systematic approach to trading by journaling your trades
Add your trades individually to a configurable and template-based trade journal or import trade history files from your brokerage account to add trades in bulk. Update the journal details in bulk as well with an intuitive excel-like grid
Improve trading decisions through in-depth reports and metrics on your trade journal
Identify your strengths and limitations by reviewing reports and metrics derived from your trading journal. Reports are generated across different categories such as portfolio, performance, risk, discipline, trading rules, and goals.
Analyze risk of option trade strategies by simulating possible future scenarios
Analyze the potential returns of option trade strategies in the future while varying stock price, implied volatility, and days to expiration. Keep updating the strategy and assessing its potential returns until you arrive at a trade that is aligned with your risk appetite.
Pick the right strategy that is aligned with your risk appetite
Let's say you are bullish on a stock. Do you know which strategy works well for you within your risk appetite? Strategy comparison feature allows you to compare trade strategies on a stock to know their profitability in different scenarios so that you can pick a strategy that aligned with your risk appetite.
Avoid repeating the same mistakes by abiding with self-set trading rules
Market volatility, ease of trading, and influx of trading ideas makes it difficult to be disciplined and influences one to take impromptu decisions. Trading Rules feature allows you to set manual or automatic rules and trade as per them to avoid past mistakes
Prepare for the day ahead with a meticulous trade plan
A trade plan created before the market hours allows you to think objectively about the market without getting influenced by the price movements. You can record your perspective on the market and intended actions during the day in a trade plan. It can be updated after the market hours to check actual actions against the planned actions
