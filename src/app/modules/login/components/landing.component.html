CueTrade is ideal for individual traders and investors who seek a disciplined, rules-based approach to trading. Whether you're a beginner aiming to improve your trading strategies or an experienced trader looking for advanced analytics and journaling tools, CueTrade helps you stay organized, make data-driven decisions, and continuously refine your trading performance.

Trading rules in CueTrade are customizable guidelines that you set to manage your trading activities.


CueTrade offers two types of rules: Automatic and Manual. Automatic rules are consistently verified for each trade, with CueTrade promptly indicating whether a trade complies with the rule or not. On the other hand, manual rules act as text-based reminders, requiring manual updates as needed.


Trading rules help you maintain discipline, avoid impulsive decisions, and improve your overall trading performance.

CueTrade simplifies trade journaling by allowing you to journal at the time of trading or anytime afterward. You can easily add notes, tags, and attach relevant files, like chart images, directly to your trades.


This organized approach ensures that your trade history is well-documented, making it easier to review past trades, identify patterns, and learn from your trading experiences.
