CueTrade is a trading platform designed to help retail traders make disciplined and informed decisions by integrating advanced analytics, trading journals, and automated rule enforcement. Its core mission is to help users avoid impulsive mistakes, preserve capital, and improve financial outcomes by promoting thoughtful, structured, and strategic trading practices.

CueTrade is ideal for individual traders and investors who seek a disciplined, rules-based approach to trading. Whether you're a beginner aiming to improve your trading strategies or an experienced trader looking for advanced analytics and journaling tools, CueTrade helps you stay organized, make data-driven decisions, and continuously refine your trading performance.

Trading rules in CueTrade are customizable guidelines that you set to manage your trading activities.


CueTrade offers two types of rules: Automatic and Manual. Automatic rules are consistently verified for each trade, with CueTrade promptly indicating whether a trade complies with the rule or not. On the other hand, manual rules act as text-based reminders, requiring manual updates as needed.


Trading rules help you maintain discipline, avoid impulsive decisions, and improve your overall trading performance.

CueTrade simplifies trade journaling by allowing you to journal at the time of trading or anytime afterward. You can easily add notes, tags, and attach relevant files, like chart images, directly to your trades.


This organized approach ensures that your trade history is well-documented, making it easier to review past trades, identify patterns, and learn from your trading experiences.


You can import historical trades from brokerage accounts into CueTrade as well with automatic and manual integration options.

Yes, CueTrade allows you to connect and manage multiple brokerage accounts from a single platform. This feature streamlines your trading process by consolidating all your trade history in one place, making it easier to track your performance across different accounts.

CueTrade presents an extensive range of statistics and reports across various categories, enabling traders to gain valuable insights into their trading performance. With a continually growing list of over reports, CueTrade ensures comprehensive coverage of essential analytics across performance, risk management, commissions, and discipline.


These insightful reports are generated based on your trading history and journal details, offering a comprehensive analysis of your trading activities.

Absolutely. CueTrade is designed to support traders at all levels. For beginners, it provides a structured approach to trading with customizable rules and easy-to-use journaling tools. The platform’s analytics help beginners understand their trading habits and improve their strategies over time.

CueTrade's Payoff Analyzer tool is an invaluable resource for analyzing option-based trade strategies, allowing you to assess their potential profit or loss at a specific future date. With the ability to input a desired stock price and adjust variables like implied volatility and days to expiration, you gain insights into how these adjustments impact potential outcomes. By understanding how adjustments to the trade strategy affect potential profit or loss, you can align your trading approach with your risk tolerance and return expectations.
