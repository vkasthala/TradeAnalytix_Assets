How it Works?

Journal Trades

3
Review Insights
  • Review stats, reports and insights derived from your trading history
  • Add or update trading rules based on the lessons learnt
  • Plan the next trading day based on the reports and insights

Frequently Asked Questions
Who should use CueTrade?
CueTrade is meant for all those traders who struggle to maintain discipline. Indiscipline includes trading without rationale, excessive trading, taking excessive risk, lack of control over emotions, etc. It helps such traders adopt a systematic approach to trading by allowing them to create a detailed trading plan before the market opens and reviewing their trades with reports and insights after the market closes so that they can identify their mistakes and learn from them. Also, it offers the ability to set rules so that traders can align their trades with the self-set rules.
When should one use CueTrade?
CueTrade can be used both before and after the market hours. It can be used before the market hours to prepare for the day ahead by creating a trading plan and analyzing the potential trades from rationale, risk and statistical perspective to make an informed decision.
After the market hours, it can be used to write notes on the trades imported from your brokerage account and review the analytics derived from your trading history. Based on the insights, one can update their trading rules and determine the actions to be taken the following trading day
What makes CueTrade easy-to-use and intuitive journaling software?
Our objective is to make journaling simple and easy to use. We understand that providing data for each and every trade is a tedious task and that is why most traders do not journal their trades despite knowing its benefits. CueTrade saves you from most of the manual entry by auto-calculating certain parameters of your trades so that you don't have to provide this data.
With CueTrade, one can provide notes on multiple trades in a single excel-like intuitive user interface, i.e., you don't have to open each trade to update its journal details rather multiple trades can be updated from a single page.
What are trading rules in CueTrade?
CueTrade allows you to set trading rules based on your prior experience or best practices. The objective of trading rules is to help you to trade within the boundaries you set for yourself and avoid repeating the same or common mistakes.
There are two types of trading rules within CueTrade: automatic and manual. Automatic rules are checked for every trade strategy added to CueTrade automatically and indicated as aligned or non-aligned. Manual rules are text-based reminders - you need to indicate manually whether a trade strategy is aligned with the manual rule or not.
How is CueTrade different from other trade journals?
CueTrade is built with retail traders in mind and is different from other journals in many regards.
  1. Our trade journal is designed to help the trader think through their trade from rationale, statistical, risk, and emotional perspectives
  2. We allow users to set up automatic and manual trading rules so that they can make sure their trades are aligned with these trading rules
  3. CueTrade groups all the trades on a symbol into a trade strategy. This allows users to enter notes, assess risk, review stats, and check alignment with rules at the trade strategy level instead of trade level.
  4. With CueTrade, one can provide notes on multiple trades in a single excel-like intuitive user interface, i.e., you don't have to open each trade to update its journal details rather multiple trades can be updated from a single page.
  5. Our emphasis on risk management differentiates us from others. Our Payoff Analysis tool allows you to analyze the P&L of trades involving options at a specific date in the future by varying stock price and implied volatility.
 What kind of statistics and reports are available in CueTrade?
CueTrade provides statistics and reports across multiple categories including performance, risk management, commissions, goals, and discipline.
How is the payoff analysis tool in CueTrade different from other option payoff tools?
Payoff analysis feature allows you to build trade strategies involving options and analyze the P&L of the strategy at a specific point in the future at a specific stock price and implied volatility levels. User can update the stock price, implied volatility and days to expiration of the options to see its impact on the P&L. With this information, users can decide whether to go ahead with the trades in their current form or modify them so that they are inline with their risk appetite and return expectations. The biggest benefit of Payoff Analysis in CueTrade is its simplicity and easy to use.
How can one add trades in their brokerage account to CueTrade automatically?
Trades can be imported from your brokerage accounts either automatically or manually. If your brokerage account is in this list, you can connect CueTrade to your brokerage and the trade history will get imported from your brokerage account to CueTrade every day automatically. For all the other brokerages, trade history files from brokerages should be downloaded and imported manually to CueTrade to add your trades in bulk.
Can I place trades from CueTrade?
No, you cannot place trades from CueTrade. CueTrade is a journaling and analytics software that should ideally be used before or after market hours.
Is CueTrade free to use?
CueTrade is in beta mode and is free to use. We will inform you and give you enough time if and when we make it a paid version.
Which markets are currently supported by CueTrade?
At present, we support US and Indian markets.
What asset classes does CueTrade support?
We support stock and options currently but are in the process of adding future contracts as well.
